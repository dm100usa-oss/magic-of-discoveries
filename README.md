# Magic of Discoveries

Official site of Magic of Discoveries LLC, an independent family publisher
in Miami, Florida:
[magicofdiscoveries.com](https://www.magicofdiscoveries.com)

The publisher is run by Ricardo Demi and Maria Demi. This site is the
catalog of twenty-seven published children's books in English and Spanish:
coloring books, step by step drawing books and bedtime stories for ages 1
to 8, plus easy coloring for teenagers and adults. It also holds free
printable pages for parents and no prep directed drawing materials for K-2
teachers, childcare and homeschool families.

Every illustration in every book is drawn by hand. The publisher does not
use generative AI to produce artwork.

The site runs in English and Spanish, each edition written for its own
reader rather than machine translated. Built with Next.js, deployed on
Vercel.
